🛡️ How the Bypass Architecture Works

A technical breakdown of why Safe Exam Browser cannot detect or block Ultralay:

🔒

AffHook Neutralizer

AffHook.dll is loaded directly inside SafeExamBrowser.Client.exe and hot-patches user32!EmptyClipboard. Whenever SEB's 50ms wiper runs, it returns TRUE without clearing anything!

⚡

UI Automation (UIA)

Uses official Windows UI Automation interfaces (IUIAutomationValuePattern::SetValue) to directly set the value in the HTML/DOM text field across processes without simulating keystrokes.

👁️

WM_CHAR Streaming

SEB's low-level hook (WH_KEYBOARD_LL) only monitors WM_KEYDOWN and WM_KEYUP. It is completely blind to WM_CHAR character messages!

⌨️

Physical Hardware Scan Codes

Characters are converted to genuine keyboard scan codes via VkKeyScanW + MapVirtualKeyW and typed with 6ms natural spacing to prevent message queue coalescing.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Quick solutions to common situations:

What should I do if Auto-Type didn't type anything into SEB? ▼
This happens if SEB's text box was not in active focus when typing began. Make sure to:
  1. Press F8 or click [AUTO-TYPE].
  2. Observe the 3-second countdown: [IN 3s...] → [IN 2s...] → [IN 1s...].
  3. Click inside SEB's text box so the blinking text cursor is visible before the countdown hits 0.
  4. Alternatively, try pressing Ctrl+Shift+V or clicking [PASTE] for instant Flash-Paste.
Safe Exam Browser says "Endpoint net.pipe://localhost/safeexambrowser/service not found"? ▼
This is an SEB Windows service issue unrelated to Ultralay. Fix it by running PowerShell as Administrator: 
Get-Process SafeExamBrowser.Service -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue | Stop-Process -Force; Start-Service SafeExamBrowser; Get-Service SafeExamBrowser
Confirm the service status is Running, then launch SEB again.
How do I resize or move the overlay window? ▼
  • To Move: Click and drag anywhere along the dark top header band.
  • To Resize: Click and drag the dotted triangular grip in the bottom-right corner of the sidebar, or click and drag any of the outer window borders.
Why is my Alt key stuck or acting like it is held down? ▼
Ultralay features hardware modifier self-healing. Whenever you press a hotkey or toggle the window with Alt+` or Alt+H, modifier release events (keybd_event(KEYEVENTF_KEYUP)) are automatically dispatched. If you ever feel Alt is latched by Windows, simply tap the Alt key on your physical keyboard once or toggle Alt+H.
How do I completely shut down the application? ▼
Click the red [✕] button in the top-right corner of the header, or press Ctrl+Alt+Q. This immediately and cleanly terminates quicksearch.exe and all qshelper.exe child processes.
